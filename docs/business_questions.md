# Business Questions

## Revenue
- How much revenue is generated over time?
- What is the average order value?
- Which states/categories drive revenue?
- Which months show meaningful growth or contraction?

## Customers
- How many unique customers are there?
- What share are repeat purchasers?
- Which customers are high value?
- Which segments are at risk?

## Retention
- What does month-1/month-2 retention look like by cohort?
- Do newer cohorts retain differently from older cohorts?

## Operations
- How long does delivery take?
- Which regions/sellers show delivery problems?
- How is delivery delay associated with review score?

## Marketplace
- Which sellers contribute the most revenue?
- Which categories have high revenue but weak customer experience?

## ML
- Can we build a defensible time-window churn label?
- Which customer attributes are predictive?
- What intervention could plausibly improve retention?
